## Findings

### Diff-scope review (cross-story)

**DR-1 · Medium-high · One tenant's slow checks stall monitoring for every tenant, and the worker turns unhealthy.** This was reproduced on the running stack (Behavior verification):
- The integration suite leaves 644 monitors behind, in 587 test organizations. They target `203.0.113.10`, an address that never answers, with a 10 s timeout.
- The compose worker checked all of them. One monitor pass logged `run_due_checks took 481468ms`, and the health check reported `unhealthy: loop monitor has not completed a pass for 475959 ms; the limit is 120000 ms`.
- A new organization's two monitors, created at 06:31:54 UTC with a 30 s interval, waited behind that queue. They were first checked at 06:47:54 and 06:48:25, 16 minutes later.

The cause is the shape of the monitor loop:
- `startLoop` single-flights `runMonitorPass`, which awaits every organization's checks (`src/worker.ts:253-298`, `:337`).
- The checks share one process-wide cap of 10 (ARCHITECTURE 6.0.1).
- Nothing limits monitors per organization (`monitor-limits.ts` bounds only interval, timeout and threshold).

So a pass takes about (queued checks ÷ 10) × timeout: 500 × 10 s ÷ 10 ≈ 500 s, against the 481 s observed. One tenant could do the same with about 200 monitors on a 60 s timeout: a 20-minute pass for everyone.

Story 5.9 met this and deferred it (`deferred-work.md:209-214`, triage B1/V-o1). That entry says the delay is "up to its timeout (at most 60 s)". The bound is wrong: it holds for one slow check, not for a queue of them. The owner's Epic 8 deferral rested on that bound.

This is inside the epic's goal ("checks run on schedule", `epics.md:1445`) and ARCHITECTURE 6.0.1's own aim: "one organization with many monitors cannot exhaust ... the network for everyone else". The story criteria did not cover it.

*Instance:* fix, with the owner's decision on the rule first (action item 1), before acceptance by the owner's decision. *Prevention:* a deferral that rests on a bound states the arithmetic behind it (action item 8).

**DR-2 · Low · The two draft paths disagree about archived services.**
- Story 5.6 rejected E2, "A service archived while its check is in flight still gets state events", with this reason (`spec-5-6-*.md:153`): "5.10 and 5.15 consumers read the service themselves".
- 5.10 does: recomputation skips archived services (`recompute-service-status.integration.test.ts:680`).
- 5.15 does not: `create-draft-from-monitor.handler.ts` never reads the service, and `insertDraftIfAbsent` (`incident.repository.ts:105-143`) checks only other incidents.
- Story 5.16's reconciler does filter them (`monitor.repository.ts` `listAtThreshold`: `m.enabled and s.archived_at is null`).

So a check in flight when its service is archived can still open a critical draft through the event, while the reconciler would never open one. The window is one check timeout. No test covers it: no case in `monitor-draft.integration.test.ts` or `record-check-result.integration.test.ts` archives a service. Boundary: 5.6 against 5.15/5.16.

*Instance:* defer (action item 2). *Prevention:* a triage rejection that relies on a later story names that story in `deferred-work.md`, so the later story's spec sees it (action item 8).

**DR-3 · Low · Partition retention does not wait for a failed rollup.** ARCHITECTURE 6.1 (`ARCHITECTURE.md:538`) says rollups "run before partition maintenance, so a day is rolled up before retention drops its raw results". That holds only when the refresh succeeds:
- `runWorkerPass` runs partition maintenance whatever `orgFailed` says (`src/worker.ts:186-231`).
- `check-result-partitions.integration.test.ts:559` and `:583` pin that maintenance still runs.

An organization whose refresh fails on every pass for `CHECK_RESULTS_RETENTION_DAYS` (30) loses those days. They are logged as "lost to retention", not kept.

Related: the `SECURITY DEFINER` drop function takes any retention of at least 1 day from `watchdog_app`. See `create_check_results.sql:214` and `check-results-env.ts:9-13`, both with minimum 1. The append-only revoke on `check_results` therefore does not stop the app role dropping every month but the current one. ARCHITECTURE 6.1 decided the argument is passed in, so a floor is a genesis decision.

*Instance:* defer to Epic 8, and correct the ARCHITECTURE sentence (action item 6). *Prevention:* none beyond the doc fix.

**DR-4 · Low, recorded · A result in flight is applied to the monitor's current row.** An edit, a disable and re-enable, or an archive that lands while a check runs still counts the old result (`record-check-result.handler.ts`, `monitor.domain.ts` `applyCheck`). The specs rejected this three times as unlikely:
- spec-5-6 B1: "needs a re-enable within one check timeout"
- spec-5-6 E3: last write wins, by a frozen decision
- spec-5-8 B1/E1: "5.9 may revisit with its loop"

Story 5.9's spec has no row revisiting it. DR-1 makes the window wider than these rows assumed: a check can now start minutes after it was queued. *Instance:* re-weigh it with action item 1. *Prevention:* covered by item 8.

**DR-5 · Low, recorded · The target guard leaves NAT64 and CGNAT open.** ARCHITECTURE 6.5 (`ARCHITECTURE.md:722`) lists the ranges the rule does not classify, including NAT64 (`64:ff9b::/96`) and carrier-grade NAT (`100.64.0.0/10`). A hostname whose AAAA record is `64:ff9b::a9fe:a9fe` reaches `169.254.169.254` on a host with a NAT64 gateway. The omission is a recorded decision (spec-5-1 B4/B5/E6/E7), but no reason is given for it. *Instance:* fix before production exposure, by the owner's decision (action item 4).

**DR-6 · Low · The check-time guard uses the same one-try resolver as configure time.**
- `guardHost` falls back to `defaultLookup` (`guarded-connect.ts:122`), which is `new Resolver({ timeout, tries: 1 })` (`target-safety.ts:279`).
- One dropped DNS packet is therefore a failed check. A hostname known only to `/etc/hosts` never resolves at check time either.
- ARCHITECTURE 6.5 says this of "the configure-time resolver" only, then "the connect-time guard is the guard".

*Instance:* defer (Epic 8), and correct the ARCHITECTURE sentence (action item 6).

- An organization deleted mid-pass fails its rollup step with `23503` at `error` level. This was seen 11 times during the integration run, as spec-5-11 B8/E2 predicted.
