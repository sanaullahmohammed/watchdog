### Diff-scope review (cross-story)

The three lenses were adversarial, edge-case and verification-gap. Their reviews were weighted to the hops 4.3 → 4.4 → 4.5/4.6 → 4.7/4.8 → 4.9.

- **DR-1 · A resync can leave every open public stream stale for up to an hour.** *Defer: action item 3.* The failure crosses 4.4 and 4.6.
  - Story 4.4 makes the listener re-listen after a lost connection and send `resync` to every subscriber, so the streams can catch up on anything missed (`notify-listener.ts:132-150`, `realtime-hub.ts:98`).
  - Story 4.6 turns that resync into one composition per organization. When a composition fails with anything but `NotFoundException`, the error is logged and the members stay, with no retry (`public-page-push.ts:110-115`). Spec 4.6 :99 calls this the designed behaviour.
  - A composition right after a reconnect is exactly the one most likely to fail: Postgres may still be restarting or the pool may be exhausted.
  - When it fails, the streams keep sending heartbeats on the pre-outage page. They recover only when a later public event arrives, or when `PUBLIC_STREAM_MAX_LIFETIME_SECONDS` (3,600 s) runs out.
  - No session saw both halves: 4.4's tests assert that the resync arrives (`notify-listener.integration.test.ts:398`), and 4.6's assert that a failed composition is logged (`public-status-stream.integration.test.ts:1366`).
  - Four other stale-page paths share the same one-hour bound, and each was accepted on its own:
    - an event between the opening snapshot and the subscribe (spec 4.6 :188, triage #17);
    - a rename (deferred 4.6 #3, `deferred-work.md:260`). The verification-gap lens adds that a *name* change, not only a slug change, emits nothing, and that no test renames through Better Auth's `organization/update`;
    - uptime bars, which are not live by decision (`ARCHITECTURE.md:383`);
    - a lost NOTIFY (Constraint 10, `epics.md:2235`).
  - Prevention: a single retry gated on the ETag would close this path. So would a low-frequency safety recomposition, which would also cover the other four. Either is cheap, because a page equal to the last one sent is never sent.
- **DR-2 · The listener forwards three free-text fields unchecked.** *Defer: action item 4.*
  - `parseNotification` checks `eventName` against the bridged set and `orgId` against the Better Auth id shape. It accepts any string for `aggregateType`, `aggregateId` and `occurredAt` (`notify-listener.ts:67-74`) and relays them to admin subscribers.
  - A forged notification needs a session as `watchdog_app`, and RLS does not apply to `NOTIFY`, so on this hop the tenant boundary is a convention.
  - The guard: an allow-list for `aggregateType`, a bounded id shape, `Date.parse` on `occurredAt`, and a sampled warning for each dropped payload (`:110-118` logs the first 256 characters of each one).
- **DR-3 · `drained()` could re-send an older page.** *Defer: action item 4.* This is latent, not reachable today.
  - `offer()` sends directly when the member is writable and leaves `state.pending` set. A later `drained()` would then send the older pending page, because its ETag differs (`public-page-push.ts:86-93`, `:169-174`).
  - It cannot happen today, because Node clears `writableNeedDrain` and emits `'drain'` in the same synchronous step. It would become reachable if a second writer or an asynchronous drain path were added.
  - The guard is one line: clear `pending` in `transmit`.
- **DR-4 · Streams opened during shutdown end at once.** *Defer: action item 4.*
  - `hub.close()` runs in `preClose` (`src/server/plugins/realtime.ts:28-30`). A stream opened after that still composes a page, writes it, and then ends, because the hub is closed.
  - `EventSource` reconnects straight away, so for the length of the shutdown each retry costs one composition.
  - The guard: a `closing` flag that answers 503 with `Retry-After` before composing.
- **Re-confirmed, already deferred to Epic 8:**
  - 4.6 #5: a resync starts a composition for every organization at once.
  - 4.8 #9: access re-checks fire in lockstep, with no jitter.
  - 4.7 #1 and #2: there is no per-user socket cap and no keep-alive, and a socket whose subscriptions have all completed stays open.
  - A reader that never drains holds its place until its lifetime ends. This is the owner's 4.6 decision that a slow reader is never cut off.
- **Dropped after re-checking the source:**
  - "A demoted member keeps admin-only signals." Admission takes any role on the ladder and no role minimum exists (`organization-events.resolver.ts` has no role comparison), so a demotion within the ladder takes nothing away.
  - "Composition runs before admission." That order is recorded and intended (spec 4.5 :192: "a 404 still wins over a 429").
  - "The streamed uptime contradicts ARCHITECTURE." `ARCHITECTURE.md:383` says "The uptime bars are not live".
  - "The NOTIFY payload can exceed 8,000 bytes." Every bridged id is bounded today.
  - "The visibility whitelist is checked against a restated catalog." That is the repository's pattern, kept on purpose (spec 4.4 triage #17).
  - The CI `compose` job's fixed project name: GitHub-hosted runners are isolated, and AGENTS.md records the minimum Compose version.
  - A resync-contract wording nit.
